# Initial Historical Strategy Research Findings — 2026-09-04

**Important:** this file records public-source evidence and structural observations. It is not a table of backtested returns. Any numerical profitability result remains blank until measured against market/shadow data.

## Stronger source anchors found

### GamingAlm — Top-100 verification and execution methods

Kicker's December 27, 2022 profile identifies Jan `GamingAlm` Bergmann as having been in FIFA22's Top-100 FUT trader list. The article documents flipping and SBC-solution/`Tech Avion` methods and explicitly accounts for EA's 5% tax when deciding a buy price.

Use: high-quality evidence that these methods were actually associated with a verifiable high-transfer-profit trader. It still does not prove a universal historical win rate.

Source: https://www.kicker.de/fifa-23-ohne-echtgeld-an-die-fut-spitze-trading-tipps-von-profi-gamingalm-920072/artikel

### FUTWIZ / TygrrFUT — dated FIFA22 clubstock and TOTW investing

The December 12, 2021 clubstock guide describes buying into reward supply, holding SBC-useful fodder and exiting into content demand. The December 19 TOTW guide documents near-discard/current-TOTW scarcity reasoning and SBC demand after cards leave packs.

Use: repeated, timestamped strategy/mechanism evidence from the same cycle. Selected examples/claimed targets remain anecdotal until reproduced from price history.

Sources:
- https://www.futwiz.com/news/article/fifa-22-clubstock-guide-12th-december/7168
- https://www.futwiz.com/news/article/fifa-22-totw-13-investing/7172

### FC24 TOTY — supply-heavy entry / expensive-card selloff logic

A January 10, 2024 FUTWIZ guide recommends staying liquid before TOTY, describes heavy pack supply lowering player prices, and discusses high-rated/TOTW investment during the release.

Use: dated FC24 evidence for promo-supply crashes, fodder entry and rebuy/rebound hypotheses.

Source: https://www.futwiz.com/news/article/how-to-get-free-packs-and-make-coins-for-toty-in-fc-24/7446

### FC26 — recurring reward/OOP/fodder mechanisms still discussed

Team Gullit's FC26 market guide describes Thursday reward supply, inform scarcity after leaving packs, early-cycle meta demand, and SBC-driven fodder cycles.

Use: current-cycle mechanism evidence. Statements such as a method being “safe” or “best” are not imported as measured performance.

## Failure/decay evidence matters

### FC25 failed high-rated fodder anticipation

A February 23, 2025 Reddit discussion records users who positioned for an expected Icon SBC demand spike and instead saw high-rated fodder fall because the released requirements did not consume the anticipated ratings.

Use: catalyst-failure example for SBC anticipation. It is stored as contradictory evidence rather than deleted because the strategy narrative failed.

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/EAFC/comments/1iwhi3d

### FC26 rating behavior is not assumed to equal older cycles

A November 18, 2025 Reddit discussion around FC26's large number of player SBC releases notes that some rating bands, particularly 84s, were not appreciating in the way users expected from older cycles.

Use: structural/decay research prompt. Community discussion alone does not establish the cause.

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/EASportsFC/comments/1p04k0m/

## Documented structural breaks

### FC24 Evolutions

EA's FC24 Ultimate Team Deep Dive introduced Evolutions. Any Evolution-anticipation strategy has no direct FIFA22/23 equivalent and therefore cannot inherit old-cycle evidence mechanically.

Source: https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/pitch-notes/news/fc-24-ultimate-team-deep-dive

### FC25 Evolution capacity

EA's January 31, 2025 Features Update increased active Evolution slots to two and updated starter packs. This can alter eligibility demand timing and early/new-club supply.

Source: https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-25/news/fc-25-pitch-notes-features-update

### FC26 repeatable/GK Evolutions and market-access changes

EA's FC26 FUT Deep Dive documents repeatable Evolutions, goalkeeper Evolutions and graduated Transfer Market access across platforms, along with broader FUT structure changes.

Use: explicit temporal-validity input for Evo, early-market/liquidity and related strategies.

Source: https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-fut-deep-dive

## Current conclusion

There is enough cross-cycle evidence to justify treating several named mechanisms as **research features**, especially:

- flipping/below-market acquisition;
- SBC solution/requirement demand;
- fodder/clubstock cycles;
- reward/promo supply buying;
- out-of-pack scarcity;
- promo panic/rebound hypotheses;
- Evo-driven eligibility demand from FC24 onward.

There is **not** enough evidence in the current repository to label their FC26 PC profitability as proven. The next step is event-aligned FC26 backtesting and shadow execution with real acquisition/sale conditions.
